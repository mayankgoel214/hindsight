    About & data

    Hindsight is a concept tool. It is not affiliated with SharkNinja. Everything on this site is built from public, free sources.

    What it is

    A DFMEA-oriented search over every public product-safety record for the small-kitchen-appliance categories SharkNinja's Ninja NPD team ships: pressure & multi-cookers, air fryers, blenders, ice-cream & frozen-drink makers, coffee & espresso, kettles, countertop ovens & toasters, grills.

    Type a part or concept; the AI translates your words into a search vector, we score it against 5,179 records, and group the matches by failure mode so you can read them as patterns rather than one-off incidents.

    Where the data comes from

    • CPSC recalls — verified U.S. regulator actions.
    • Health Canada recalls — verified Canadian regulator actions.
    • EU Safety Gate — verified EU alerts.
    • SaferProducts.gov consumer reports — unverified consumer claims, always labeled as such in the UI. They are useful signal, not proof.

    Open government data; each record on this site links back to its source so you can read the original.

    What "unverified consumer report" means

    Anyone can file a report on SaferProducts.gov. The report is published after a short review but it is not independently investigated. Hindsight shows these reports because they are the largest signal of real-world consumer use, but it never counts them as recalls, and it labels them "unverified" on every surface they appear in.

    Bring your own data later

    The record schema (hazard text, failure mode, cause, effect, quote, source) is the same shape as a DFMEA row. The pipeline accepts your own FMEAs, DVT/EMC results, field returns and lessons-learned — once that private data lands, every search covers your history too. Public data makes the demo useful on day one.

    Design notes

    • Every claim links to a record. "No matching record" instead of guessing.
    • SharkNinja records appear in the same neutral format as every other brand.
    • Patterns show regulator actions (recalls + safety alerts) and unverified consumer reports as separate numbers, never combined.
    • Everything runs client-side after one search; the server is only there to embed the query.
